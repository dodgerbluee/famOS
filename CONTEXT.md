# famOS

Family operating system for one household: people, shared screens, chores, and tasks.

## Language

**Adult**:
A family member with the admin or parent role. Adults are the household's primary accounts.
_Avoid_: user (when you mean an adult), main account, admin (when you mean any adult)

**Kid**:
A family member with the kid role. Kids have chores and household currency; they are not household admins.

**Kiosk**:
A shared household device (wall tablet, kitchen screen), not a person. It stays signed in until an adult revokes it.
_Avoid_: kiosk user, kiosk account, admin (when you mean the wall tablet)

**Family member**:
A person in the household — an adult or a kid. Kiosks are not family members in the people list.
_Avoid_: user

**Pairing**:
Attaching a physical device to a kiosk identity, typically by scanning a QR code.
